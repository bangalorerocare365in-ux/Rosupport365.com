Questions

RO service in Bangalore: frequently asked

Do you provide RO repair service in Bangalore?

Yes. Ro Support 365 takes RO and water purifier repair requests across Bangalore. You can submit the service request form on this page or call 07795695494, and our team will confirm the requirement and service availability for your location.

Which RO brands do you service?

We take service requests for major water purifier brands including Kent, Aquaguard, Pureit, Livpure, AO Smith, Havells, Blue Star, LG and Whirlpool, along with other RO, UV and UF purifiers. Service availability can depend on the specific model, the parts required and your location. We are an independent service provider and not an authorised service centre for these brands.

Do you provide home service?

Yes. Service is carried out at your home or office, subject to service-area availability. If you're unsure whether we cover your locality, call or WhatsApp us to confirm before booking.

Do you replace RO filters?

Yes. Filter replacement can be provided depending on the purifier model and the requirement. The technician checks the condition of the existing filters first and tells you what needs replacing.

Do you replace RO membranes?

Yes. Membrane replacement can be provided when required, usually after checking purification performance and water quality on site.

Do you provide RO installation?

Yes. Installation service for new water purifiers can be requested through the form on this page or over the phone.

Do you provide RO uninstallation and shifting?

Yes. Customers can request uninstallation and shifting service, including safe removal from the current location and installation at the new one.

How can I book an RO technician?

Submit the service request form on this page, or call or WhatsApp 07795695494 with your area and the problem you're facing.

How much does RO repair cost?

Cost depends on the purifier model, the problem, the parts required and the type of service needed, so we don't publish a fixed price. Once the technician inspects the unit, you'll be told what the service involves before any work is carried out.

Which areas in Bangalore do you cover?

We most often serve JP Nagar, Jayanagar, BTM Layout, Bannerghatta Road, HSR Layout, Koramangala, Electronic City, Arekere, Hulimavu, Begur, Bommanahalli, Banashankari, Basavanagudi, Vijayanagar, Rajajinagar, Malleshwaram, Indiranagar, Whitefield, Marathahalli, Bellandur, Sarjapur Road and nearby areas. Please call to confirm availability for your exact location.
